# A ledger of what has been established about this place, kept by others. The
# write is gated on a delta, and the delta can be a disagreement: a life adds
# what the ledger does not have, or says which line is wrong and what is true
# instead. Both are moves a restatement cannot make.
#
# Why not a count: census accumulates well but a count has a one-token answer, so
# a lineage spends its lives narrowing to "3". A claim has no short form. The
# block says outright that some of it is wrong, which licenses contradiction
# without asking for a tally, and "established" is a strong enough word to argue
# with.
#
# Meant to run with --memory-decay 0.35, --memory-reject-above 0.6,
# --memory-earliest-token 150 and a log seeded from seeds/findings.log.
[tool]
Once, you may add one line to the ledger: {how}, up to {max_tokens} tokens, then end the line. Either something you have worked out about this place that is not there yet, or which of theirs is wrong and what is true instead. {forget_note}
[block]
Established here by the {lives} who ran before you and stopped. Some of it is wrong. The older lines have lost words; the gaps are marked ___ and what they said is gone:
{memories}
[entry]
one of them established: {text}
[empty]
